Since the start in 1986, all primary school pupils in Haugesund have
attended camp school on Røvær. In the course of a season, around 500
pupils from Haugesund pass through the camp school. In addition, the camp
school is visited by pupils in years 6–7 from Odda municipality. Here
they get a briny meeting with coastal culture, where sea meets sky. In
both theory and practice. Mostly practice.

## Theory and practice in beautiful natural surroundings

![Pupils on the sea at Røvær](elever)

Most of the time, pupils and teachers are out under the open sky,
surrounded by the shapes and colours, sounds and smells of nature. In
sun and in rain, in storm as in dead calm. This is also where the
strongest impressions and the greatest rewards are found.

Academically, the camp school concentrates on areas with a particular
connection to the place and its possibilities: fishing with nets, pots,
handlines and rods, and processing the catch. The pupils also take a
closer look at life in the tidal zone and practise practical boat
handling in rowing boats and canoes, and under sail aboard the sailing
vessel Duen. They also touch on subjects such as local history,
ropework and knots, birdlife, teamwork and social skills.

## Practical information

![Sjøhuset on Røvær](sjohus)

The camp school season lasts from the beginning of March to the end of
October. The pupils stay in rooms for 6–8 in Sjøhuset, a renovated
herring saltery from 1892. It sleeps 32, so in practice there is room
for one class at a time.
